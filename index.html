The Weight of This Day
فَضْلُ يَوْمِ عَرَفَة
Why This Day Stands Apart

Every year, on the ninth of Dhul Hijjah, something extraordinary takes place. Here is why the scholars call it the greatest day the sun has ever risen upon.

Allah's Greatest Promise
On no other day does Allah set free more souls from the Fire than on this day. The gates of mercy swing open in a way unlike any other moment in the year.
"There is no day on which Allah sets free more people from the Fire than the Day of Arafah."
Sahih Muslim
Religion Perfected
The final and greatest verse of the Quran — completing the divine message — was revealed on this very day. It was the day Allah announced His pleasure with Islam as a complete way of life.
"Today I have perfected your religion for you, completed My favour upon you, and chosen Islam as your religion."
Al-Ma'idah 3
The Day of Duā
The Prophet ﷺ stood on the plain of Arafah, one hand holding his camel's rein, the other raised to the sky — making duā from Dhuhr until Maghrib. This is the day that transforms supplication into certainty.
"The best duā is the duā of the Day of Arafah."
Tirmidhi
Two Years of Sins Erased
Fasting this single day carries a reward unlike any other voluntary fast. For those not on Hajj, it is a complete purification — past and future.
"It expiates the sins of the previous year and the coming year."
Sahih Muslim
The Key Window
From Dhuhr to Maghrib — approximately six hours — this is the peak of the peak. The pilgrims are gathered on the plain. The angels descend. Allah draws near. Pour everything you have into these hours.
★ 12:30 PM – 6:30 PM IST
Be Fully Present
This could be your final Arafah. The companions understood this and wept on this day. What would you regret not asking? What would you wish you had said to your Lord? There is still time.
"Make much duā on the Day of Arafah, for I have not seen a day greater than it."
Imam Malik
Before The Day
كَيْفَ تَسْتَعِدّ
How to Ready Yourself
01
Free Your Day
Cancel non-essentials. Protect Fajr to Maghrib. This day is not for productivity or socialising — it is for Allah alone. Tell people you are unavailable. You will not regret it.
02
The Night Before
Sleep early tonight. Make your niyyah with full sincerity. Ask Allah to give you a present, soft heart tomorrow — one that is ready to ask, to weep, and to receive.
03
Fast — It Erases Two Years
The Prophet ﷺ confirmed this fast expiates the sins of the previous year and the year to come. Prepare a light suhoor. Keep your body ready for a full day of worship.
04
Silence Your Devices
Mute notifications. Remove the noise. This day does not need your reels, your stories, or your group chats. Let it be between you and Allah — and only that.
05
Write Your Duā List
Use our planner below. Write every request now — don't leave it to the moment. Think of every person you love, every need you carry, every dream you've been afraid to ask for.
06
Know What to Say
Have your duas ready. Have the Names of Allah ready. Have your words of praise prepared. We have gathered everything you need in the sections below — go through them tonight.
07
Set Your Intention
One clear niyyah: to give this day everything you have. No holding back. No half-presence. When you wake for Tahajjud, know that you woke for Allah — and Allah does not disappoint the one who comes to Him.
